05 / THE PROPOSAL

A considered scheme.
The detail behind it.

The supplied project summary describes a full planning application for six dwellings on land adjacent to Rose Cottage, Main Street, North Muskham.

APPLICATION REFERENCE 25/01920/FUL

Newark & Sherwood District Council
Application received: 11 November 2025

Planning approval is not confirmed in the supplied information.

Design, landscape & arrival.

Materials & architectural character

Russet stock brick, horizontal timber cladding, red pantiles and anthracite roofing, with heritage-style casement windows and conservation rooflights.

Access & courtyard

The proposal relocates the existing access slightly south, with a shared-surface access road and a private gravel courtyard serving Plots 2–6.

Landscape & biodiversity

Brick garden walls, estate fencing and native hedging, with integrated bat, swift and sparrow boxes described in the project summary.

Parking & everyday provision

The summary records 15 proposed parking spaces, including one visitor space, EV charging to each plot and bin and cycle stores.

Site area & drawing information

The summary records 3,800 m² in the application form and approximately 4,350 m² on the layout. The site area requires confirmation; no definitive area is advertised here.
